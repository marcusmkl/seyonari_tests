Very Good Music Videos

These are music videos/discography of artists that I would recommend to anyone. Their music is just very good in my opinion and that it conveys a lot of emotions which I resonate with.

UNDERSTAND - keshi

A love song about unexpectedly finding a deeply significant partner, feeling a strong sense of completion, and the singer's willingness to take the vulnerable risk of deep love despite being scared.

Yours To Keep - grentperez

This song serves as a devotion to a beloved partner and a declaration of selfless love, portraying his heart as no longer solely his but the other person's. The song encapsulates the album's themes of yearning, insecurity, and loss, culminating in a profound statement of lasting love and commitment.

Chest Pain(I Love)

This song refers to the bittersweet pain of a love that is lost but still lingers in the heart, a feeling he aimed to capture with a smooth yet piercing musical production. The title combines the idea of emotional distress, often felt as "chest pain," with the enduring emotion of love
