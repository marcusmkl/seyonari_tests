Pork Adobo

Pork Adobo is a classic Filipino dish that is made by frying pork and simmering it in soy sauce and vinegar over time. It has many different variations based on how you cook it, but this recipe covers the classic or normal Pork Adobo recipe that many Filipinos love.

Ingredients

  • A Kilogram of Pork Kasim or Liempo
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Water
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Bay Leaf

Instructions

  1. Put some oil in a pan and saute the garlic and onions.
  2. Then, add the pork and saute until it is cooked.
  3. Add soy sauce, vinegar, and pepper to the pan
  4. Adjust seasoning and taste to your liking. Also add a bay leaf
  5. Simmer until the pork is done and you may now serve it with piping hot rice.
